How it works

Each simulation is a scenario that can be benign or malicious. You will be presented with a decision (click link, login, open attachment, scan QR, answer the call, etc.). Choosing incorrectly will animate an attack timeline and show real-world consequences (account compromise, money loss, data leak). Choose correctly and you’ll get a full breakdown of why you were safe.
