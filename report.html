🚨 Report a Cybercrime

If you are a victim of any cybercrime, you must raise a complaint on the official Government of India Cybercrime Portal.

Steps to File a Complaint:

  1. Go to the official portal: cybercrime.gov.in
  2. Click on “File a Complaint” from the homepage.
  3. Select the type of complaint (e.g., Women/Child Related or Other Cyber Crimes).
  4. Read the advisory and click “Accept” to proceed.
  5. Login using your mobile number and OTP verification.
  6. Fill in the complaint form with complete details (incident, date, screenshots, evidence).
  7. Upload supporting files (screenshots, messages, links, etc.).
  8. Submit the complaint and note down your reference number for future tracking.
